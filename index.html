What I'm into

  • Linear algebra. My favourite theorem: if $T$ is a self-adjoint operator on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$, then $V$ has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors of $T$, i.e. $$T = \sum_{i} \lambda_i P_i ,$$ where the $\lambda_i$ are the (real) eigenvalues and the $P_i$ are the orthogonal projections onto the eigenspaces.
  • Differential geometry. I am currently working on Gaussian curvature.
  • Formalising mathematics in Lean. I'm working through Mathematics in Lean, turning proofs I know on paper into proofs a computer can check.
  • Making maths visual and interactive. I like building small tools that let you see what a definition means.
